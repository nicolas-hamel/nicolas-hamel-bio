A platform for a Unified Control Centre, not one isolated application

Panorama Suite is an OT/IT Data Management Platform for Unified Control Centres. SCADA is one of several applications supported by the platform, alongside GeoSCADA, BMS, EMS, Historian, PSIM and MES. The same foundation can bring these applications together in a vendor-independent Unified Control Centre.

This distinction matters for mixed estates. An operator may need to retain existing PLCs, RTUs, BMS controllers, meters, security subsystems and specialist applications while standardising data, alarms, history, reporting and operator workflows. The platform approach creates a consistent operational layer without making the software commercially dependent on one equipment range.

Applications and sectors in the East African context

The first application should follow the operational need, not a predetermined product label. Potential routes include:

  • SCADA and GeoSCADA: monitoring, control and geospatial operations for energy, water, transport and distributed infrastructure;
  • BMS and EMS: unified building operations, energy performance and resource visibility across sites;
  • Historian and MES: production context, traceability, quality, maintenance and enterprise data exchange;
  • PSIM: operational and physical-security information brought into a common control-centre view;
  • Unified Control Centres: several operational applications, sites and equipment families coordinated through a common data and engineering model.

East African opportunities span energy and utilities, water, transport, buildings, data centres, manufacturing, mining and other distributed infrastructure. Every project still needs an installed-base assessment, protocol validation and a clear division of operational and cybersecurity responsibilities.

Design the regional delivery model with the architecture

East Africa combines fast-growing cities, cross-border infrastructure and geographically distributed assets. Connectivity, local skills, procurement models and lifecycle-support requirements vary by country and site, so the software architecture and partner model should be designed together.

Partner structure

Define distribution, integration and support roles

Agree what ZEK, CODRA, integration partners and the customer own across commercial coordination, engineering, commissioning, first-line support and escalation.

Local autonomy

Keep essential operations close to the process

Define what must continue during WAN or service loss, how long a site must operate independently and how data is reconciled after connectivity returns.

Interoperability

Validate the real installed base

Confirm protocol versions, drivers, gateways, timestamps, alarm semantics, redundancy and ownership of engineering files before committing to scale.

Multi-site design

Central visibility without central fragility

Use Edge-to-Service patterns where local applications need autonomy alongside central hypervision, administration, reporting and controlled services.

How to start a Tanzania or East Africa programme

  1. Define the operational outcome: the decisions, alarms, control actions or performance improvements the first scope must support.
  2. Map the installed base: equipment families, protocols, current applications, data quality, networks and lifecycle constraints.
  3. Set the application boundary: decide whether the first deliverable is a SCADA, GeoSCADA, BMS, EMS, Historian, PSIM or MES application, or a multi-application control-centre layer.
  4. Agree delivery ownership: identify CODRA, ZEK, integrator and customer responsibilities for engineering, testing, cybersecurity, support and knowledge transfer.
  5. Validate a focused use case: confirm interoperability, operator usability, resilience and support processes before scaling across sites.

Frequently asked questions

Who is CODRA's distributor in Tanzania?

CODRA lists ZEK Group International in Tanzania among the distributors in its international Panorama network. ZEK Group International is headquartered in Dar es Salaam.

What does the ZEK Group International partnership add for Tanzanian operators?

It creates a Tanzania-based commercial route to CODRA's Panorama Suite platform and partner ecosystem. Distribution, integration, commissioning and lifecycle responsibilities can then be organised with CODRA and the appropriate project partners.

Is Panorama Suite only a SCADA product?

No. Panorama Suite is an OT/IT Data Management Platform for Unified Control Centres. SCADA is one of several applications supported by the platform, alongside GeoSCADA, BMS, EMS, Historian, PSIM and MES.

Does an East African Panorama Suite architecture require cloud connectivity?

No. Essential applications can remain local and autonomous. Central or cloud services can be added selectively for reporting, administration, analytics and multi-site visibility when the operating model and cybersecurity assessment justify them.
