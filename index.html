Shared vocabulary

Unified Control Centre, hypervision or integrated operations centre?

A Unified Control Centre is a common operational environment that consolidates data, alarms, visualisation, history and workflows from multiple OT, IT, building and security systems. Depending on the sector and country, a similar architecture may be described as a unified or integrated operations centre, a hypervision platform, a manager of managers or an umbrella system. These expressions overlap, but are not always technically identical.

Primary website term

Unified Control Centre

This is the consistent cross-sector umbrella term used on this website. CODRA's official wording uses the US spelling, Unified Control Center; this site otherwise follows South African and UK English.

Operations terminology

Unified or integrated operations centre

These terms are common in industry, utilities, mining and multi-site operations. Operations control centre, central control centre and command and control centre are also used in particular sectors.

Integration terminology

Hypervision, manager of managers and umbrella system

Hypervision is widely used for the layer above several supervision systems, especially in Francophone, building and security contexts. Manager of Managers describes a federating architecture; umbrella system is a useful but informal expression.

Related, narrower concepts

PSIM, IBMS, SOC, NOC and DCIM

These labels describe particular sectors, functions or operating teams. They can form part of, connect to or provide a starting point for a Unified Control Centre, but they are not universal synonyms for the complete cross-domain environment.

Terminology FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Unified Control Centres

What is a Unified Control Centre?

A Unified Control Centre is a common operational environment that consolidates data, alarms, visualisation, history and workflows from multiple OT, IT, building and security systems. It can support several applications and sites while preserving the required local autonomy.

Is a Unified Control Centre the same as a Unified Operations Centre?

The terms overlap, but they are not guaranteed to be technically identical. Unified Operations Centre and Integrated Operations Centre are common in industrial, utility, mining and multi-site contexts, while Unified Control Centre is the primary cross-sector term used on this website.

What is the difference between supervision and hypervision?

Supervision usually operates one process, system or application. Hypervision sits above several supervision or specialist systems to provide a consolidated view, cross-system context and coordinated workflows. The precise boundary depends on the project.

What does Manager of Managers mean?

Manager of Managers describes a federating architecture that aggregates information and alarms from multiple underlying management systems. It does not by itself guarantee the control, historian, workflow, engineering or lifecycle capabilities of a complete Unified Control Centre.

How is a Unified Control Centre different from PSIM, IBMS, SOC or NOC?

PSIM, IBMS, SOC, NOC and DCIM describe particular sectors, functions or operating teams. They can form part of, connect to or sometimes provide the starting point for a Unified Control Centre, but they are not universal synonyms for the complete cross-domain environment.
