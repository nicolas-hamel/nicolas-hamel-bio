Operational value

One data platform, many operational applications

My work focuses on vendor-independent architectures that preserve local control, connect existing assets and give operators a durable path from individual applications to a Unified Control Centre.

Independence

Freedom from equipment-level lock-in

Panorama Suite is developed by an industrial software publisher rather than a PLC or equipment manufacturer. This supports a consistent OT/IT data layer across mixed installed bases and gives asset owners more freedom in future application, upgrade and integration choices.

Interoperability

Build the application the operation needs

SCADA, GeoSCADA, BMS, EMS, Historian, PSIM and MES applications can share a common data architecture, with standard protocols and interoperability credentials including OPC UA and BACnet BTL.

Resilience

Local autonomy with central visibility

Edge-to-Service patterns keep essential applications close to the process while enabling central hypervision, administration, reporting and selected services across geographically distributed sites.

Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity backed by ANSSI qualification

Panorama E2 has received ANSSI CSPN certification and ANSSI qualification. CODRA also describes the platform as aligned with NIS2 requirements and progressing towards IEC 62443 certification.

Innovation with a use case

From distributed operations to earlier fault detection

Panorama Edge-to-Service

For water networks, utilities, buildings, transport and industrial sites that need autonomous local operation, reliable data transfer and centralised engineering across a multi-site estate.

Automatic Fault Detection with AI

CODRA's integrated AFD component learns normal historical operating patterns, accounts for operating phases and alerts teams to abnormal behaviour. The intended value is earlier diagnosis, predictive-maintenance support and better operational and energy performance.

Regional focus

Four African operating contexts

The technology foundation can be common, but delivery, languages, connectivity, installed bases and local partner models vary. Regional context belongs in the architecture and the delivery model.

West Africa

Nigeria distribution, direct CODRA access elsewhere

Felton Energy Services represents CODRA in Nigeria only. Other West African markets engage through Johannesburg or suitable certified integration partners.

Central Africa

Direct CODRA and certified partner route

Johannesburg coordinates commercial and technical engagement, with certified integrators selected in-country or from a neighbouring market.

East Africa

Tanzania distribution, direct CODRA access elsewhere

ZEK Group International represents CODRA in Tanzania only. Other East African markets engage through Johannesburg or certified integrators.

Southern Africa

CODRA's Johannesburg office and ecosystem

Direct access to CODRA's Africa business and technical office, supported by certified integration partners across Southern African markets.
