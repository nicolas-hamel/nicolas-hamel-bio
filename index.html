Operational value

One data platform, many operational applications

My work focuses on vendor-independent architectures that preserve local control, connect existing assets and give operators a durable path from individual applications to a Unified Control Centre.

Independence

Freedom from equipment-level lock-in

Panorama Suite is developed by an industrial software publisher rather than a PLC or equipment manufacturer. This supports a consistent OT/IT data layer across mixed installed bases and gives asset owners more freedom in future application, upgrade and integration choices.

Interoperability

Build the application the operation needs

SCADA, GeoSCADA, BMS, EMS, PSIM, MES, historians and other operational applications can share a common data architecture, with standard protocols and interoperability credentials including OPC UA and BACnet BTL.

Resilience

Local autonomy with central visibility

Edge-to-Service patterns keep essential applications close to the process while enabling central hypervision, administration, reporting and selected services across geographically distributed sites.

Cybersecurity

Evidence-based security positioning

CODRA reports active, uninterrupted ANSSI CSPN certification for Panorama E2 and an ANSSI qualification history. Its public roadmap also describes alignment with NIS2 requirements and a progressive IEC 62443 certification approach.

Regional focus

Four African operating contexts

The technology foundation can be common, but delivery, languages, connectivity, installed bases and local partner models vary. Regional context belongs in the architecture and the commercial plan.

Southern Africa

Industrial depth and critical infrastructure

South Africa and neighbouring markets, with strong mining, water, energy, transport, buildings and data-centre use cases.

East Africa

Fast-growing distributed operations

Multi-site utilities, infrastructure, buildings and industrial projects where phased deployment and local autonomy matter.

West Africa

Mixed estates across large territories

English- and French-speaking partner ecosystems supporting energy, water, industry, transport and public infrastructure.

Central Africa

Resilient, maintainable architectures

Projects where remote support, skills transfer, lifecycle planning and intermittent connectivity must be considered from day one.
