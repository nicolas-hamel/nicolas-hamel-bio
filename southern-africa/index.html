Executive summary

Southern Africa combines mature industrial estates, long-lived automation assets, geographically distributed infrastructure and demanding cybersecurity and lifecycle requirements. Panorama Suite provides a vendor-independent OT/IT Data Management Platform that can connect mixed equipment and applications through a common Unified Control Centre foundation.

Direct regional access: CODRA's Johannesburg office is the business and technical coordination point for African customers and partners. Projects can then be organised with a suitable certified integration partner in the customer country or a neighbouring market.

This gives operators direct access to the software manufacturer while retaining the local engineering, commissioning and lifecycle capabilities required for project delivery.

A platform for a Unified Control Centre, not one isolated application

Panorama Suite is an OT/IT Data Management Platform for Unified Control Centres. SCADA is one application supported by the platform, alongside GeoSCADA, BMS, EMS, Historian, PSIM and MES. These applications can share a common operational data and engineering foundation.

This matters where brownfield PLCs, RTUs, meters, BMS controllers, security subsystems and specialist applications still have useful operating life. A vendor-independent platform can retain these assets while standardising data, alarms, history, reporting and operator workflows.

Applications and sectors in the Southern African context

The first application should follow the operational outcome. Relevant routes include:

  • Mining and industry: SCADA, Historian and MES applications across mature, mixed-vendor production estates;
  • Energy and water: control, geospatial operations, energy performance, historical analysis and distributed infrastructure;
  • Transport and public infrastructure: resilient operational applications and Unified Control Centres across critical sites;
  • Buildings and data centres: BMS, EMS, resource-performance and multi-site applications;
  • Security: PSIM applications combining alarms, video, access control and operational context.

Design the delivery model for long-term operation

Southern African operators often need to integrate legacy assets without shortening their useful life, while improving cybersecurity, supportability and visibility across sites. The project model must therefore address both initial delivery and long-term ownership.

Brownfield integration

Validate the real installed base

Confirm protocols, drivers, gateways, alarm semantics, timestamps, redundancy and ownership of existing engineering files.

Cybersecurity

Define zones, conduits and accountability

Set the remote-access, identity, patching, backup, recovery, logging and incident-response model before commissioning.

Lifecycle

Plan support beyond handover

Agree training, documentation, source ownership, version management, first-line support and escalation across CODRA, the integrator and customer.

Multi-site design

Keep local autonomy with central visibility

Use Edge-to-Service patterns where local applications must continue independently while central teams need hypervision, administration and reporting.

How to start a Southern African programme

  1. Contact the Johannesburg office: outline the country, operating need, project stage and required local capabilities.
  2. Map the installed base: equipment families, protocols, current applications, data quality, networks and lifecycle constraints.
  3. Define the application boundary: identify the first SCADA, GeoSCADA, BMS, EMS, Historian, PSIM, MES or Unified Control Centre scope.
  4. Select the delivery model: agree the CODRA, certified integrator and customer responsibilities for engineering, testing, cybersecurity and support.
  5. Validate a focused use case: confirm interoperability, operator usability, resilience and lifecycle processes before scaling.

Frequently asked questions

How can a Southern African operator engage CODRA?

Operators can engage CODRA directly through its Africa business and technical office in Johannesburg. CODRA can then help organise the appropriate commercial, technical and integration route for South Africa or a neighbouring country.

Who delivers Panorama Suite projects in Southern Africa?

Projects can be delivered with a suitable CODRA-certified System Integrator Partner in the customer country or, where appropriate, a neighbouring country. Engineering, commissioning, support and escalation responsibilities are defined for each project.

Is Panorama Suite only a SCADA product?

No. Panorama Suite is an OT/IT Data Management Platform for Unified Control Centres. SCADA is one application supported by the platform, alongside GeoSCADA, BMS, EMS, Historian, PSIM and MES.

Can Southern African operators retain existing automation equipment?

Yes, subject to protocol and driver validation. Panorama Suite is designed as a vendor-independent operational data platform that can integrate mixed installed bases without requiring every controller or field device to come from one manufacturer.
